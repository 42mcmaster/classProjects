## Slide 2 — "The Master Program"

**Must-include facts (exact):**
- The **operating system** is the master software that manages the whole computer.
- The three functions **every** OS shares: (1) **manage resources** — CPU, memory, drives, peripherals; (2) **provide a user interface**; (3) **run and service applications**.
- The always-wrong answer: **no OS is 100% hack-proof**.

## Slide 3 — "OS Vocabulary That Gets Tested"

**Must-include facts (exact — all five, defined precisely):**
- **Boot**: starting the OS; loads all the drivers. **Driver**: small program that lets the OS and a device communicate. **Daemon**: starts at boot, runs as a background process to support multitasking. **Kernel**: core of the OS; manages memory, CPU, and hardware-software communication. **Access Token**: holds a login session's security credentials — the user, their groups, their privileges.

**Go deeper:**

**PowerPoint requirement:** a **table** (Insert > Table): Term | What it does — all five terms.

## Slide 4 — "The OS Lineup"

**Must-include facts (exact):**
- **Windows** — Microsoft, desktop, proprietary. **macOS** — Apple, desktop, proprietary. **Linux** — community, desktop, **open source**. **iOS** — Apple, mobile, proprietary. **Android** — Google, mobile, **open source**.
