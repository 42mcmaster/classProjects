## Slide 3 — "Memory vs. Storage — Don't Mix Them"

**Must-include facts (exact — the most-tested idea in this domain):**
- Types of **MEMORY**: **RAM, ROM, Flash ROM**. Types of **STORAGE**: **SSD, HDD, USB Flash Drive**.
- **RAM**: working memory while running; **volatile** (contents lost at power off); more RAM = more speed for gaming/multitasking.
- **ROM**: holds start-up instructions to load the OS; **non-volatile**.

**Go deeper:**
- Find the RAM and storage amounts of one actual device your team uses (a lab PC, your phone) and put the real numbers on the slide — labeled correctly as memory or storage.

## Slide 4 — "Picking the Right Storage"

**Must-include facts (exact):**
- **SSD**: no moving parts → fast, durable; costs more per GB. **HDD**: most storage for your money; moving parts → bump-prone. **USB Flash**: small, portable, durable; less capacity.
- The worked scenario: *"room for thousands of photos"* → SSD, HDD, or USB Flash (because they're **storage**, never RAM/ROM).

**Go deeper:**
