# Slide Squad: Build the Class Study Deck for Technology Basics

**Team project · Your team becomes the expert on one slice of IC3 Lesson 01 — and builds the PowerPoint slides the whole class will study from.**

---

## Your mission

Your team gets **one sub-lesson** from Lesson 01: Technology Basics (01a through 01f). The slides I gave you for that sub-lesson are *bare bones* — just the exam facts. Your job is to rebuild them as **real PowerPoint slides**: same must-know facts, but expanded, explained, illustrated, and presented like you actually understand them. Because you will.

At the end, all six decks get combined into **one class study deck** for the IC3 Level 1 exam. If your slides are weak, the whole class studies weak slides. No pressure.

This is also your **first PowerPoint project**. Every slide has specific PowerPoint skills you must use — these are real skills from the Microsoft Office Specialist (MOS) PowerPoint certification, so you're starting that path too.

## What your team builds

**One PowerPoint deck, exactly 6 slides:**

| Slide | What it is |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Title slide** — sub-lesson name, team member names |
| 2–5 | **Your 4 content slides** — see your team's Spec Sheet for exactly what goes on each |
| 6 | **Sources slide** — where your facts and images came from |

Your team's **Spec Sheet** lists, for each content slide: the **must-include facts** (these are the IC3-tested facts — get them *exactly right*), a **go-deeper requirement** (something you research beyond the packet, like history or real-world context), and which **PowerPoint skill** that slide must show off.

## The PowerPoint requirements (every team, every deck)

These match the demos we'll do in class — nothing here you won't be shown first:

1. **A design theme** applied to the whole deck (Design tab). Pick one your whole team agrees on. Readable beats flashy.
2. **Correct layouts** — Title Slide layout for slide 1, Title and Content layouts for the rest. Don't build slides out of floating text boxes.
3. **A bulleted list with at least two indent levels** (on the slide your Spec Sheet marks).
4. **A table** (on the slide your Spec Sheet marks) — built with Insert > Table, not drawn with lines.
5. **At least one picture**, inserted, resized *without squishing it*, given **alt text**, and with its source on your Sources slide.
6. **Slide numbers and a footer** with your team name — on every slide *except* the title slide (there's a checkbox for that; find it).
7. **One transition** applied to **all** slides. One. The same one. Consistency looks professional.
8. **One entrance animation** on your quiz-answer reveal (see your Spec Sheet) — the answer must stay hidden until you click.
9. Deck saved as `DL1_[your sub-lesson]_[team names].pptx` — e.g., `DL1_01c_Jordan_Sam.pptx`.

## The content rules

- **Packet facts must be exact.** Your Study Guide is the source of truth for definitions and terms — the IC3 exam tests these *precisely*. Don't "improve" a definition until it's wrong.
- **In your own words everywhere else.** Explanations, examples, and your go-deeper slide content should sound like you, not like a copy-paste.
- **Go-deeper facts need a credible source** — a real organization or news site, with an author or a date you can point to. It goes on your Sources slide.
- **Slides are cue cards, not essays.** Short phrases on the slide; the full explanation comes out of your mouth when you present.

## Presenting

Your team presents your 4 content slides to the class (~4–5 minutes). **Every team member talks.** End with your quiz question: ask the class, take answers, *then* click to reveal — that's what the animation is for.

While other teams present, you're not off duty: their slides are your study material for the Lesson 01 quiz and the IC3 exam. Take notes.

## How you're graded (50 points)

| What | Points |
|---|---|
| Must-include facts: all present and exactly correct | 15 |
| Go-deeper research: done, credible source, explained in your own words | 10 |
| PowerPoint requirements: all 9, checked one by one | 15 |
| Presentation: every member spoke, quiz moment ran properly | 5 |
| Sources slide complete (facts + images) | 5 |

*Your deck joins the class study deck only after it passes the fact check. Errors come back to you for fixes — the class doesn't study wrong answers.*
