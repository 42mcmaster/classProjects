## Slide 2 — "What Software Actually Is"

**Must-include facts (exact):**
- **Software** = the instructions that tell the hardware what to do; it has **no physical form**.
- The two big families: the **operating system** and **applications**.
- The download chain: **downloading** (online source → your device) is not the same as **installing** (putting an executable on your device, usually via a wizard); **uploading** is the opposite of downloading; **subscribing** = paying monthly/annually for cloud software.

## Slide 3 — "Application, App, or Web App?"

**Must-include facts (exact):**
- **Application**: performs a **variety of functions**. **App**: **single purpose, one function** — a lightweight version, typically for phones/tablets.
- **Desktop application**: must be **installed** before it runs. **Web-based application**: runs **from the Cloud** (examples: Google Apps, Microsoft 365, Gmail).

**Go deeper:**
- Pick one program that exists in BOTH forms (e.g., PowerPoint installed vs. PowerPoint on the web) and list two real differences you found by opening both.

**PowerPoint requirement:** an inserted **picture** (e.g., a phone home screen of apps, or screenshots of the two versions) — resized without distortion, **alt text** added, source cited on slide 6.

## Slide 5 — "Free to Change vs. Owned (Quiz the Class)"

**Must-include facts (exact):**
- **Open source**: source code anyone can view/modify; generally **free**; support = community forums; examples **Linux, Android**.
- **Proprietary**: code owned and not shared; **sold commercially** (NOT usually free); official support; examples **Windows, macOS**.
