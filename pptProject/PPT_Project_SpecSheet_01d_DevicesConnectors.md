## Slide 4 — "Display, Audio & Network Ports"

**Must-include facts (exact):**
- **VGA**: video only ("Display Only"). **HDMI**: video **and** audio — one cable does both. **RJ-45**: wired Ethernet network data. **3.5mm jack**: audio only.
- The worked scenario: which single cable carries both picture and sound to a TV? **HDMI**.

**Go deeper:**
- Inspect an actual classroom/lab machine (or the projector cart): which of these four ports does it actually have? Report what you found.

**PowerPoint requirement:** a **table** (Insert > Table): Port | What it carries | Where you found one.
